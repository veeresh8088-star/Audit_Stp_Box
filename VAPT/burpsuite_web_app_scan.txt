Burp Suite Professional - Web Application Security Scan
Target: https://172.201.152.88/api/login

Issue: Missing Secure/HttpOnly Flags on Session Cookies
Severity: Medium
Confidence: Certain
Description: The web application sets session cookies without the 'Secure' and 'HttpOnly' flags. Attackers can steal these cookies via Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) or man-in-the-middle attacks.
Remediation: Configure all session cookies with the 'Secure' and 'HttpOnly' flags in the Set-Cookie headers.
